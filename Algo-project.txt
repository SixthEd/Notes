Sorting:

Selection Sort: find min and then swap.

Bubble Sort: Push max to the last by adjacent Swapping.

Insertion Sort: take a element and put its correct position by backward adjacent swapping.

Merge Sort: Divide and merge.

Quick Sort: choose pivot and put it on its correct position in the array. Smaller on the left  and larger on the right side (Divide and Conquer).
 
Radix Sort: Sort the number using digit wise.
